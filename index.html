Solution architecture

How RetinaXplain will work

01

Image-quality gate

Check focus, illumination, and retinal field of view. Poor images are flagged for immediate recapture.

02

AI screening

A future validated model will grade No DR through Proliferative DR and identify referable cases.

03

Clinical explanation

Grad-CAM maps and lesion evidence will help ophthalmologists review AI decisions quickly and safely.

04

Referral workflow

High-risk patients are prioritized for tele-ophthalmology review and treatment referral.

Built for SIH 2026

Problem Statement 26038: Explainable AI for Diabetic Retinopathy Screening in Rural India.

Planned datasets: APTOS 2019, IDRiD, DRIVE, and Messidor-2. Planned model modules: image-quality assessment, CLAHE enhancement, lesion segmentation, five-level grading, calibrated confidence, Grad-CAM, and human-in-the-loop clinical review.
